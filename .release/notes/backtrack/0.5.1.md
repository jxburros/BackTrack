# BackTrack 0.5.1

## Changes

### Added

- **Degradation augmentation** for training (`training/augment.py`): `--augment room,noise,mic,compress,clip,wow,codec`
  (or `all`) and `--augment-copies N` on `train_drums` and `train_pitched` train on degraded copies of
  every training item as well as the clean ones, so models also work on phone, live and rehearsal
  recordings, old tapes and low-bitrate MP3s. Copies are made once before the feature cache, each under
  its own key, and keep the labels' timing. Off by default: runs without it are unchanged. The pipeline's
  `train.augment` turns it on, and `evaluate.bad_recordings` scores a set of real bad recordings beside
  the clean test split in the report.

The Surveil binary, its page and the desktop apps are unchanged from 0.5.0.
